Someone is calling you through VOXA. During this call, VOXA listens to what you say and checks it for scam patterns (for example, requests for OTPs or urgent payments) to protect the person who invited you.

  • Your voice goes directly to the other person's browser.
  • Your speech is transcribed by a speech-to-text service while the call is active, then discarded. No audio is recorded or stored.
  • The person who invited you may end the call, and VOXA may end it automatically if strong scam evidence is heard.
