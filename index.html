A clearer view

Two different questions.
One place to review them.

Authenticity and intent tell different stories. A genuine voice can carry a scam; synthetic speech can be harmless. VOXA keeps those findings separate.

01 / UNDERSTAND

You stay in control

Consent, clear boundaries,
and a shorter data trail.

Fraud-intent rules run on your device. Transcription and authenticity checks use the providers you select. API use may consume provider credits.

Permission before processing

Confirm consent before microphone recording or sending media to a provider. Live sessions show when the microphone is on.

Raw media stays temporary

VOXA processes uploads in memory. Selected providers receive the media and apply their own retention policies.

Reports on your terms

Keep reports in this tab session, or opt into 24-hour local retention. Export what you need and delete reports and alert history.

Browser session restoration may restore a tab. Expired local reports are purged while the app is running or on next use. This workspace uses a local demo session, not a hosted account system.
